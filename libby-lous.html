Our Story

A little shop
with a lot of soul.

Libby Lou's was born out of a love for slow mornings, warm mugs, and the kind of place where you actually want to linger. Tucked into the heart of Covington, KY, we're your neighborhood spot for craft coffee, seasonal drinks, and homemade bites — made with care, served with a smile.


Whether you're here to work, catch up with a friend, or just breathe for a minute — you belong here.
